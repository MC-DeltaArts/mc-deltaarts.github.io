About me

Hi! I'm Marco, also known as Delta online. I'm from Italy and I'm a Master's graduate in Computer Science from the Politecnico di Milano, where I've learned most of the technical, social and problem-solving skills that have gotten me where I am today.

While my head's always been good with numbers and code, my heart has always been one of an artist, so it's been my life's objective to find something that would allow me to pursue and improve in both, while finding ways to connect one to the other.

I've been playing games since I was little, and every story I uncovered only made me fall deeper in love with the medium. Nowadays, I can proudly tell people that I help create art, in the hopes it will inspire someone else to follow the same path.

In three words I am:

Dependable

I'm no stranger to working under pressure and will always deliver my best quality work in the time I'm given. Sacrificing sleep? Eh, I've done worse in college.

Adaptable

I always seek new opportunities to learn and improve, mess around with new technologies or throw myself at something I've never attempted before... like this website!

Human

I make mistakes, but all the work I do is mine. If I'm given a solution, I have to understand it first, because my creations are what makes me, me.

Let's get in touch!

I'm actively seeking opportunities for new games, prototypes, and projects to work on and help turn into realities, so if you think I could be a good fit for your team, don't hesitate to reach out!
