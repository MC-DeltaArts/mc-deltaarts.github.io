About Me

Hi! I'm Marco, also known as Delta online. I'm from Italy and I'm a Master's graduate in Computer Science, attended at the Politecnico of Milan, where I've learned most of the technical, social and problem solving skills that have gotten me where I am today.

While my head's always been good with numbers and code my heart has always been one of an artist, so it's been my life's objective to find something that would allow me to pursue and improve in both - while finding ways to connect those with either inclination to one another.

I've been playing games since I was little, and every story I uncovered only made me fall deeper in love with the medium. Nowadays, I'm can proudly tell people that I help create art, in the hopes it will inspire someone else to take my same path.

In three words I am:

Dependable

I'm no stranger to working under pressure and will always deliver my best quality work in the time I'm given. Sacrificing sleep? Eh, I've done worse in college.

Adaptable

I always seek new opportunities to learn and improve, mess around with new technologies or throw myself at something I've never attempted before... like this website!

Human

I make mistakes, but all the work I do is mine. If I'm given a solution, I have to understand it first, because my creations are what makes me - me.

Let's get in touch!

I'm actively seeking opportunities for games, systems development, and general IT. If you think I'd be an asset to your team, don't hesitate to contact me!
